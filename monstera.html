Monstera

Monstera deliciosa

Toxic to pets
LIGHT

Bright indirect light

WATER

Every 1–2 weeks

HUMIDITY

50–70%

TEMPERATURE

18–27°C

Monstera is a tropical plant with large green leaves. It likes bright indirect light and regular watering. With proper care, its beautiful leaves can grow very large and bring a tropical atmosphere into your home.

Care instructions

Light

Place the Monstera in bright indirect light. Avoid strong direct sunlight.

Water

Water when the top layer of soil becomes dry. Do not leave the plant standing in water.

Humidity

Monstera prefers higher humidity. You can occasionally mist its leaves.

Temperature

Keep the plant in a warm room. Avoid cold drafts and sudden temperature changes.

Care tips

  • Water when the soil is dry.
  • Give the plant bright indirect light.
  • Clean the leaves regularly.
  • Rotate the plant from time to time.
